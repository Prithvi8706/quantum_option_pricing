A further candidate, **not a known result supplied by these papers**, is offline
compilation of a two-variable boundary into a decision diagram/staircase and
coherent lookup. For ideal positive stocks,
`w1*exp(x1)+w2*exp(x2)<H` can be written
`x2<log((H-w1*exp(x1))/w2)` only where the logarithm argument is positive.
This is a nonlinear boundary, not a threshold on `x1+x2`. Native floor-polynomial
monotonicity and tie locations must be proved before compressing a native
boundary. The formula does not construct its lookup oracle or extend to the
multi-date Asian sum for free. Learned, tensor-network or analog loaders may be
research alternatives, but no reviewed exact native access/error/cost theorem
for them is supplied here; they are not the first test.

## Separate ideal, native and amplitude contracts

Let `Q=2^40`, and let `F(x)` be the exact nonnegative integer stock word produced
by the frozen clipped-log degree12 Estrin subgraph. CONST values are canonical
unsigned72 modular words decoded signed when used arithmetically; CMUL `c` and
saved signed coefficient tables are signed72 literals already. Every signed
product, stored word, shift and eagerly evaluated dead branch needs the same
safety contract as the source. No real exponential is silently substituted for
`F`, and no unproved native monotonicity is used.

The mathematical two-asset/one-date ideal component is
`A=(exp(x1)+exp(x2))/2`: knock-out at `A>=140`, survival at `A<140`, separate
positive payoff at `A>100`. It is a component instance, not a replacement of
the existing 4/8-asset, 12/52-date contract or its averaging literals.

For the corresponding native component, `S=F(x1)+F(x2)` and
`B_raw=floor(S/2)`. Provided exact positive sum/no-wrap premises:

* `B_raw<140Q` iff `S<280Q`; equality knocks out.
* `max(B_raw-100Q,0)>0` iff `S>=200Q+2`.
* `S=200Q+1` still has zero native payoff. Using only `S>200Q` loses a tie.

These are algebraic integer identities, not executed numerical tests. For full
B4/B8 Asian means use the actual saved coefficient and native floor; in particular
the B8 average literal is rounded differently from `floor(Q/(8*52))`. A toy's
one-half coefficient is not transferred to the full source.

The finite lattice also permits a different strict-predicate formulation:
compare S with the half-integer centers `280Q-1/2` and `200Q+3/2`, respectively.
Their signs classify the integer barrier and strike correctly with distance
at least1/2 in raw-sum units. In the basket signal `S/(2 M Q)` this gives a
guaranteed gap at least `1/(4 M Q)` before any further affine normalization.
This is a paper observation, not a fitted polynomial, query bound or claim that
the reachable q32 law has this worst-case gap. Any signal approximation must
preserve or pay for this gap; shifting a block encoding adds its own normalization
and access cost. Thus native ties need not imply universal polynomial
impossibility, while a convenient large spectral gap cannot be assumed.

A binary predicate implementation has a declared clean-XOR ABI on arbitrary
dirty output bits, unchanged inputs and zero scratch after cleanup. An amplitude
loader instead declares its entire unitary, signal/flag probabilities, all
garbage and inverse; ancillas need not be clean halfway through a block encoding.
Its complete source, inverse and controlled versions must have the required
relative phase. Basis probabilities alone, or unitary equality only up to an
input-dependent phase, cannot pass a controlled pricing-source contract.

## Terminal and release rules

First-test outcomes are `COMPONENT_PASS_WITH_OPEN_OBLIGATIONS`,
`COMPONENT_REJECTED`, `UNSUPPLIED_OPERATION`, `DOMINATED_AT_MATCHED_SCOPE` or
`FAILED_OR_CENSORED`. A mathematically correct toy may retain the general-access
and finance gates open. Any incomplete/censored run, failure marker or unsealed
receipt overrides success-looking partial tables, with zero credit/adoption.
All fresh receipts, copies and failure evidence are exclusive and immutable;
owned inventory plus canonical ROOT maps and scoped runtime maps are checked
against sealed original expectations and freshly verified at termination.
Mutable progress/STATUS paths are never direct frozen scientific inputs.

At most one later emission requires a separate fixed implementation release:
complete native/control/phase correctness or a source-specific approximate G2
certificate, complete cost advantage at the unchanged accuracy and cap, actual
primary and independent PASS plus ROOT integrity verification. If all gates pass,
test one additional date before extrapolating to either complete benchmark.
If a gate fails, retain its exact missing operation or dominated scope and keep
the original explicit arithmetic. There is no automatic emission, universal
no-go, advantage claim, source adoption, changed precision or changed target.

Remaining uncertainties are concrete: an economical full supported-domain
one-stock signal, native monotonicity/decision-diagram compression, finite tie
atoms and near-boundary mass, a certified threshold/payoff polynomial with
affordable queries, controlled cleanup in the project's native model, and
whole-source normalization/repetition/scheduling cost. This prospectus resolves
none of those by assuming free QRAM, free Gaussian preparation or free offline
data loading. Web access was read-only primary-literature research; all future
code, tests, outputs and receipts remain local, with no pushes or uploads.
