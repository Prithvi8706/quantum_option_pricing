# Onefold and tenfold accounting

All six actually measured C09 confirmation rows were reconciled against the
complete logical state demand. Four have the same nominal $0.01 accuracy target
as the current quantum ledger; two have the finer classical $0.001 target.
Coverage qualification and the continuous/finite-law bridge remain open.

Actual source-only and complete quantum times, speedup ratios, slowdown ratios
and tenfold budget-miss ratios remain null. Logical T-depth is not physical
reaction depth, and no archived hardware clock or qubit cap was transferred.

For compatible complete durations the exact identities are
`speedup = T_C/T_Q` and `tenfold budget miss = 10*T_Q/T_C = 10/speedup`.
Missing a tenfold target does not establish a slowdown: a speedup of two misses
that target by a factor of five while still beating the classical duration.

Twelve exact conditional supply thresholds were calculated: `N_T/T_C` accepted
protected states per second for onefold, and `10*N_T/T_C` for tenfold. These are
necessary supply conditions, not sufficient architectures or executable times.
Retries, protection, transport, routing and initial-state stock receive no free
credit. The counts and durations are bound to their actual audited receipts.

The independent auditor confirmed all six rows, twelve rates, ratio identities
and null qualifications. The first numerical packet is retained; the second
producer only splits an overlong prose literal to pass Ruff, with identical AST
and comparison results. Neither packet adds test-case, gate or price credit.
