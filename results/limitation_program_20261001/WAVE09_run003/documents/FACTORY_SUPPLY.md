# Actual state demand and factory compatibility

The fixed H05 screen and independent arithmetic audit passed: 18 fixtures,
exactly two factory methods on the actual B4 demand, and one separate H06
native-state interface. B8 supplies a hash-bound companion demand target;
no second physical factory profile was inferred.

The accepted seven-T lowering needs 511,911,060,861,915 B4 T states and
4,195,204,814,583,315 B8 T states. With a 0.001 magic-state failure allowance,
the marginal output error targets are approximately 1.95×10⁻¹⁸ and 2.38×10⁻¹⁹.
No physical factory, clock, capacity or executable latency was adopted.

For fifteen-to-one distillation, an ideal independent-Z model with perfect
Clifford operations gives a conservative finite-input-error bound
`C(15,3)*p^3/(1-p)^15`. It includes postselection rather than treating the
leading-order `35*p^3` expression as a finite-error certificate. From the fixed
input error 0.001, the three explored levels enclose errors near 4.62×10⁻⁷,
4.48×10⁻¹⁷ and 4.10×10⁻⁴⁷, with retries and raw-input consumption charged.
Only the third conservative enclosure meets the B4 target. This does not prove
that three levels are necessary or establish a device-level error bound.

One published modeled distillation row reports 4.5×10⁻²⁰ output error. That
number is arithmetically compatible with the target under its stated model;
its footprint and timing have not been transferred to this computation.
[Litinski, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.06903v3).

The cultivation comparison retains the required escape stage, the paper's
distance-three simulation proxy and its sign-entangling erratum. The explored
escaped estimates do not certify this computation's required output quality.
An ungrown low-error state is not a usable delivered output for this ledger.
[Cultivation paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.17595v1).

The H06 sensitivity separates native CCX opportunities from 1,759,815 remaining
terminal-IQFT T states. Native CCZ injection would require a real mapped
lowering with measurements and corrections. Optional catalysis needs 879,908
additional CCZ conversions and an initial catalyst; its seed, storage,
conversion faults and correlated outputs remain separate obligations.
[CCZ/catalysis paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.01238v3).

The next compatible supply calculation must verify usable output quality and
rejection/delivery behavior under one finite noise model, then fit protected
factories, data, routing and buffers together. All missing physical fields
remain null.
