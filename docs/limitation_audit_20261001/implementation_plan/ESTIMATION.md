# Estimation and certification: a local implementation plan for E01–E25

Plan recorded on 1 October 2026. This file turns all 25 entries in [the estimator audit](../ESTIMATION_AND_CERTIFICATION.md) into finite work packages. It is a plan, not a new experiment or a claim that every candidate will work. The runner should execute ready work automatically, retain passing candidates, stop branches at their declared limits and move to the next eligible task. It must preserve archived results and keep every code/result write local.

## Interfaces that every estimator must preserve

The latest B4×12/B8×52 arithmetic sources are knock-out workloads. The saved C4/H8 Hadamard and bounded schedules estimate a compound-policy residual. Their source, output, law and normalization differ. **Never multiply a favorable C4/H8 query count by a favorable B4/B8 depth.** First bind an estimator to the exact selected source and output, then count that complete pair. The new constant multiplier is retained arithmetic evidence, not an estimator speedup.

For the saved residual, the exact selector has `p=E[(Y+128)/256]`; mean error `e` needs probability error `e/256`. The saved mean allowance is `0.002/discount` and estimator failure is `0.003`. A known-moment phase estimator instead needs a proved second moment of the **identical digital residual**. Continuous lognormal moments, finite Box–Muller laws, guarded arithmetic and the implemented policy cannot be exchanged silently.

Every row needs integer source-forward/source-inverse counts, preparation per shot, phase/selector traversals, reflections, longest chain, repetitions, rotations, feedback and readout. Deterministic dollar biases and failure probabilities have separate ledgers. Their complete-price limits are the fixed `epsilon` and `0.01`; open policy, law, synthesis or physical obligations prevent a complete-price pass. Classical comparison receives the same controls and any applicable conditioning, RQMC, MLMC or adjoint improvements.

G0 fixes the task and provenance; G1 validates integer arithmetic; G2 certifies total price error; G3 supplies an explicit estimator/confidence schedule; G4 supplies the matched timed classical comparator; G5 supplies physical resource/timing and the tenfold inequality. Dependencies below identify the interfaces needed by each work package. An estimator prototype can finish before G2 or G5, but its result remains conditional until the missing interfaces pass.

## Execution order and branch boundaries

Start with E02/E03/E07 and reuse the completed E05/E06/E13 mechanisms. In parallel, evaluate E10's interval moment fallback and E22's predicate census. Then use small finite screens for E08, E21 and E24; compare E04 only against the current certified bounded schedule. E11 handles the digital moment bridge; E09/E12 are alternatives if moment acquisition remains costly. Advance to QSP or parallel AE only with explicit source, confidence and resource interfaces. E14–E19 are separate research/input/output branches and cannot change the fixed one-price benchmark without a new G0 contract. E25 cannot be promoted on posterior credibility alone.

All artifacts go to a fresh `results/limitation_program_20261001/E##_<run>/` directory. The protocol declares candidates and caps before execution. `certificate.json` records proved versus sampled evidence; `comparison.json` includes the best eligible baseline, rejected candidates and fallback; `manifest.json` hashes source, producer and outputs. Planned artifact names do not imply the files exist yet. The machine-readable [estimation.json](estimation.json) contains the same work packages.
