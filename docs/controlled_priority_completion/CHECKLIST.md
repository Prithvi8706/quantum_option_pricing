Remaining dependencies for an advantage claim, in order:

1. Exhibit a complete execution approach for this same contract that fits the
   latency and physical-qubit budgets. Current component improvements do not.
2. Certify the same digital-policy baseline and regret at their allocated errors
   and failure probabilities, paying acquisition/training costs. Certify the
   tight digital moment only if the Hadamard branch is retained.
3. Validate the physical mapping/noise/decoder assumptions and all requested
   output costs under a consistent hardware scenario.
4. Only then freeze genuinely held-out cases and classical tuning, and run the
   original robustness rule: 24 cases, at least 20 with 10x improvement and all
   at least 3x, with the matched financial and confidence contract.
