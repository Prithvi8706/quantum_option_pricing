# Publication route and submission prerequisites

Checked against official publisher pages on 22 September 2026. This is a venue
and submission-readiness assessment, not a prediction of acceptance. No new
quantum-advantage direction was searched and no submission was sent.

## Recommended article

Prepare a **Regular Article for Quantum Information Processing** as the first
submission target. Its stated remit covers quantum algorithms, error correction
and fault tolerance, which matches the technical center of this work.
[Official scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/11128/aims-and-scope).

My proposed title is **Auditable digital-oracle resources for controlled
compound Asian-basket pricing**. The contribution should be the reproducible
connection between a financial output contract, competitive controlled classical
methods, an explicit estimation schedule, the emitted reversible source, and
separately stated approximation and physical-capacity obligations. This is my
editorial recommendation, not the publisher's assessment of the manuscript.

The manuscript should make the scientific result assessable without requiring a
reader to follow the development conversation. Four particular findings are
worth presenting together:

1. Useful controls substantially reduce the classical workload on the chosen
   compound-price contract, so nesting alone does not establish a hard problem.
2. Concrete finite-confidence estimator choices change coherent-query costs by
   orders of magnitude; an asymptotic query count hides consequential constants.
3. The finite-law analytic control needs an explicit bias correction, while
   arithmetic range and expectation-error statements have different scopes.
4. Bit-exact arithmetic and parallel schedules improve the implementation, yet
   the currently justified physical mapping still does not establish the
   declared crossover. Upper schedules and conditional capacity constraints
   must remain distinguished.

These are candidate contributions, not a certified novelty claim. The existing
literature establishes the constituent control techniques, amplitude/mean
estimation ideas, reversible arithmetic identities and surface-code primitives.
A paper must identify the part of their integration that a reader can reuse,
and compare it specifically with earlier option-pricing resource estimates.
The number of source files, tests or failed crossover scenarios is not a novelty
argument. A separate independent novelty review remains necessary before
representing the article as submission-ready.

The strongest defensible headline is a reproducible digital-oracle feasibility
case study. Avoid “end-to-end quantum advantage,” “quantum advantage is
impossible,” “complete continuous-price certification,” and “hardware runtime”
unless new evidence establishes those statements. AI-agent review is useful
internal scrutiny; it does not replace author responsibility or external peer
review.

## Verified requirements and one inconsistency

The journal accepts Regular Articles of unrestricted stated length and uses
single-blind review. Supply author identities, affiliations and corresponding
contact; editable manuscript sources and compiled PDF; declarations of funding,
interests and contributions; a data-availability statement; and disclosure of
substantive LLM assistance. Human authors retain accountability. Submission also
attests originality, absence of concurrent submission and coauthor approval.
The page requests 4–6 keywords. Its specific review section requests an 80–100
word abstract, while the later generic title-page section requests 150–250.
Keep both versions ready and resolve that mismatch through the actual submission
interface or editorial clarification. Do not silently describe the website as
unambiguous. [Official author instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/11128/submission-guidelines).

The website identifies a hybrid publishing model. A publication-route/fee choice
must match the author's institution and funding, rather than being assumed from
an unverified directory. [Official publishing options](https://link.springer.com/journal/11128/how-to-publish-with-us).

ACM Transactions on Quantum Computing is a plausible computing-oriented fallback,
but its current journal-specific author pages were inaccessible in this audit.
Accordingly, no current page limit, review-anonymity rule, publication fee or
submission checklist is asserted for TQC. Its launch page is historical evidence
of a theory/practice remit, not current submission instructions.
[Official ACM launch announcement](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/press-releases/2020/december/tqc-launches.pdf).

## Concrete readiness checklist

| Item | Present status or required action |
|---|---|
| Target and article type | QIP Regular Article recommended; no editor endorsement |
| Narrow technical claim | Draft around the auditable digital source and estimator comparison |
| Novelty | Focused comparison must distinguish new analysis/artifacts from known components |
| Scientific limits | Keep financial, hardware and confirmation conditions visible in abstract and conclusion |
| Relation to September manuscript | Disclose overlap; preserve the earlier implementation-comparison claims |
| Author metadata | Obtain names, order, affiliations, contact and author-approved declarations |
| Draft and references | Prepare editable source, compiled PDF, cited primary literature and verified numbers |
| Reproduction package | Freeze the exact source/result hashes cited in the submitted draft |
| AI assistance | Describe actual substantial coding, derivation and drafting assistance; do not invent human validation |
| Submission | Requires completed article and accurate author attestations; has not occurred |

Suggested Methods disclosure for author review:

> AI coding assistants were used to develop and inspect implementation code,
> mathematical derivations and manuscript drafts. Separate AI-agent reviews were
> used as internal checks. The named human authors are responsible for verifying
> the final claims and approving the submitted version.

Use that wording only after the named authors have actually performed and
accepted the stated responsibility. Their names, affiliations, grants,
conflicts, approvals and validation work cannot be inferred from a repository
username or supplied on their behalf.
