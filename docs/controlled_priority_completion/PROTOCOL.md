# Priority-ordered completion attempt

22 September 2026. Starting point: merged PR #10, commit 15c2e1c8.
This is a new development attempt on the same controlled compound Asian-basket
candidate. The existing financial contract, controls, classical comparators,
one-cent/99% per-price target and tenfold complete-latency requirement stand.
No new advantage direction or held-out confirmation case is introduced.

1. **Cost gate first.** Construct valid parallel schedules of the existing emitted
   arithmetic, with private scratch and shared-control copy costs paid. Explore
   the depth/qubit tradeoff, a tighter justified estimator schedule, and optimistic
   hardware capacity constraints. A failed conservative upper schedule is not
   evidence of a lower bound. Separate conditional necessary capacity inequalities
   from constructed execution schedules and from hypothetical ideal query counts.
2. **Financial certificate.** Use sound integer ranges and structural elementary
   function bounds to extend or falsify the remaining arithmetic obligations.
   Any unproved branch/range, digital moment or policy-regret transfer stays open.
   Diagnostics cannot replace a guarantee. Continue bounded certificate work
   even if the cost gate fails, as authorized by the user's completion request.
3. **Physical/full-price accounting.** Apply new cost evidence consistently and
   close terms that the new evidence supports. Missing validated device/layout
   assumptions remain conditional. Do not count compilation or CPU verification
   as quantum runtime.
4. **Confirmation decision.** Run an advantage confirmation only if the preceding
   gates actually pass. Otherwise record ineligibility and its quantitative cause;
   do not relabel a development/resource study as confirmation.
5. **Publication readiness.** Prepare a claim appropriate to the results, check
   relevant prior work and venue requirements, and identify any author/submission
   information needed to finalize a submission. Preserve the original manuscript.
6. **Independent review.** Authors do not approve their own technical work.
   Separate subagents review code, proofs, resource accounting and claims after
   the new results are available; fixes receive focused verification.

Development checks use root seed 2026092801. Cached gates are verified against
their existing hashes; new schedules are executed on stored diagnostic inputs
and checked against exact integer targets. All resource sweeps and unsuccessful
attempts are reported. Finishing a work item does not imply its scientific
acceptance condition was met.
