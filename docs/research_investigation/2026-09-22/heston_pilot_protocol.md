# Heston discrete-target rejection pilot (frozen before execution)

Date: 2026-09-22. This is a development diagnostic, not confirmation or a continuous-Heston pricing result.

Reproduce Wang and Kan, Quantum 8, 1504 (2024), weak-Euler Asian instance 1: S0=100, v0=.1, r=.03, rho=-.1, kappa=2, theta=.12, xi=.3, K=90, T=1, N=256, averaging S(h),...,S(T). Each increment is an independent equiprobable sign times sqrt(h); no Gaussian approximation or Brownian bridge is used. Price the capped payoff min((average-90)+,200), discounted by exp(-.03), and separately record the uncapped mean and observed exceedances. The cap gives a precise finite-model contract; it is not a proven upper bound on the uncapped payoff. Double precision arithmetic is used. No continuous-model, time-discretization, tail or quantum-arithmetic error certification is attempted.

Methods: vectorized independent Monte Carlo and independently Owen-scrambled Sobol points thresholded at .5, in chronological order. Each point is paired with its sign-reversed antithetic. Each method uses 16 independent replicates, 4096 base points (8192 paths) per replicate. Seeds are 22092026+0,...,15 for MC and 22093026+0,...,15 for RQMC. A single independent 4096-base-point antithetic MC training sample (seed22091026) fits one control coefficient by covariance/variance: the arithmetic mean of log returns, whose discrete-model mean follows the exact first-moment recursion E[v(j+1)]=(1-kappa*h)E[v(j)]+kappa*theta*h and E[R(j+1)]=E[R(j)]+(r-E[v(j)]/2)*h. Both raw and controlled estimates are reported, with training time charged to every controlled method's total time. No selection changes, additional seeds or tuning after execution.

Output: each replicate price, across-replicate standard error, empirical two-sided Student-t 90% and 95% half-widths (15 degrees of freedom), total wall clock including random point generation and simulation, training time and machine/library context. Intervals describe variability across randomizations; finite-replicate Student-t intervals are empirical, not rigorous. There is no independent reference price; agreement between methods is a consistency diagnostic, not an accuracy certificate. Report negative variance events and cap exceedances.

Decision calculation: compare timing with the *published circuit*, Table6: 2.4e11 T gates, 1.2e11 T-depth, 2.2e4 logical qubits at normalized error1.3e-3 and 90% AE confidence, corresponding to approximately $.252 after discounting. Use effective T-layer times .1,1,10 microseconds only as sensitivity scenarios, not vendor capabilities. Classical half-width below $.252 within seconds rejects the old circuit as a practical advantage candidate at that discretized contract, even though a formal guarantee has not been matched. A future quantum design would need a new resource reduction and continuous-model error evidence; this pilot does not reject all Heston quantum algorithms.
