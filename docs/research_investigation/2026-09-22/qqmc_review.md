# Quantum quasi-Monte Carlo and the strongest classical integration challenge

Search date: **2026-09-22**. This is a bounded investigation, not exhaustive coverage. New evidence and calculations only; no manuscript or archived result was changed.

## Decision

**No defensible significant quantum advantage established yet.** Do not make quantum quasi-Monte Carlo (QqMC) the paper's primary positive claim. Retain coherent Sobol generation as a possible state/path-input construction, to be compared against structured Gaussian and random-bit constructions. Its most useful immediate contribution is a falsifiable test: determine whether the *actual best classical pricing cost* leaves enough time per fully implemented coherent oracle to permit a 10-fold quantum speedup.

The current project had already identified the QqMC possibility in `docs/journal_sprint/ADVANTAGE_RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md`; reopening it as a claimed advantage would recycle an unvalidated proposal. New evidence from reading the complete lead makes the proposal less persuasive, while recent classical preintegration and confidence methods strengthen the comparator.

Repository files inspected: that directions document; `QUANTUM_RESCUE_RESEARCH.md`; `COMMON_COMPILATION_RESULTS_20260922.md`; the September 22 manuscript's classical/advantage discussion; the September 21 comparator audit; and `research/journal_sprint/classical_baselines.py`. The latter already contains PCA paths and independently fitted geometric controls, so adding ordinary Sobol/PCA/control-variate comparisons is not a new research direction. The comparator audit explicitly acknowledges cheap conditional/controlled RQMC and unmatched finite-sample confidence. Recent work below supplies a route to repairing the confidence comparison.

## Executed checks and independent deductions

Ran [qqmc_sanity.py](qqmc_sanity.py); results are in [qqmc_sanity.json](qqmc_sanity.json). These are independent arithmetic checks, not a reproduction of the paper's quantum simulator.

For a known linear integrand f(x)=sum_j w_j x_j, integration over the unit cube gives exactly sum_j w_j/2. A single midpoint evaluation gives the same result. Any antithetic pair x and 1−x also averages to that result. Unscrambled Sobol quadrature at N=2^q instead has bias −sum_j w_j/(2N), since each coordinate's marginal is a permutation of the left-endpoint dyadic grid. All 12 checks for dimensions1,2 and q=1,...,6 agreed to 10^-14. Consequently, improving over that left-endpoint quadrature does not establish computational advantage for this family.

For a schedule K={1,2,...,2^r} consisting of powers of two and 2,048 shots per depth, let E=sum(k). Full A/A† calls equal

    N_A = 2048 × sum_k(2k+1) = 2048 × [2E + (r+1)].

Thus N_A/E exceeds4,096, before controlled-gate, reflection or fault-tolerance costs. Even the smallest schedule k=1 requires6,144 preparation/inverse calls. This check is decisive for interpreting the plotted toy comparison; it does not preclude a different rigorously analyzed QAE implementation from doing better.

Another basic logical obstacle is that B_Q < B_C for two valid upper bounds does not imply actual quantum error < actual classical error, and does not imply an optimal classical lower bound. Enlarging a loose classical variation/discrepancy bound can make that bound comparison look better without making the pricing task harder. No paper claim should be built on this inference.

For inverse-normal inputs, endpoint handling is part of the algorithm: the zero Sobol point maps to minus infinity under the normal inverse. Midpoints, finite bit randomization, truncation, or a different distribution construction require an explicit discrepancy/tail/numerical allowance. A coherent net of 2^Q labels does not produce a price for free. It only supplies inputs to repeated coherent path/payoff evaluations.

## A dimensionally consistent kill test

Measure the cheapest eligible classical total runtime T_C(epsilon,delta), including setup and all requested outputs, and compile a quantum circuit with N_A complete oracle invocations. For a stipulated 10-fold latency improvement, the allowable mean time per full invocation is

    tau_A <= [T_C/10 − T_Q,setup − T_Q,measurement/decoding] / N_A.

A nonpositive numerator immediately rejects that configuration. This is a necessary condition, not a sufficient crossover claim. Oracle duration must include inverses and workspace cleanup consistent with the invocation convention, while schedule reflections and other operations are charged separately. A physical estimate must also pass logical-failure and qubit/factory feasibility.

The saved sensitivity table contains illustrative thresholds: if classical delivery takes0.1s and quantum needs100,000 full calls, a 10-fold win permits at most100ns per full call before overhead. These values are hypothetical combinations, not measured project runtimes or a hardware prediction. They expose which missing constant must be measured.

For diagnosis, suppose over a frozen practical window the classical error behaves as A n^-alpha with cost c_C n, and quantum estimation needs kappa R/epsilon full calls of duration tau_A, where R is the dollar normalization. Ignoring setup only for this algebra,

    T_C/T_Q = c_C A^(1/alpha)/(tau_A kappa R) × epsilon^(1−1/alpha).

When alpha=1, tightening tolerance does not improve this ratio. For alpha>1 it worsens the quantum ratio; for alpha<1 it can help. This is a finite-window model requiring uncertainty and sensitivity checks, not an asymptotic extrapolation. A variance-sensitive quantum method replaces the appropriate range dependence with its actual variance/moment theorem and constants; the classical method receives the identical control and conditional information.

## Small prospective option-pricing experiment

Hypothesis to test, not recommended as a claim: for discretely monitored arithmetic Asian-basket calls at absolute errors $0.01,$0.05,$0.10 per basket initialized at100, with99% confidence, coherent-net QAE delivers at least10-fold lower conditional fault-tolerant latency than the fastest validated classical method, over a predeclared region, without a larger physical-compute budget. Tenfold is an engineering margin against uncertain constants, not a statistical-significance threshold.

Begin with exact GBM transitions at contractual monitoring dates; this avoids conflating contractual monitoring with time-discretization error. Use4/8 assets,16/64 monitoring dates, moneyness0.8/1/1.2, volatility0.2/0.4, correlation0/0.3/0.8, maturities1/2. Repeated strikes are a separate amortized workload; time all outputs and controls on both sides. Do not call a move to CVA a pricing continuation.

Development: select12 cases before timing, with independent held-out12 cases drawn from the same parameter grid using fixed seed2026092201. Tune only on development data. Sample sizes2^8,...,2^14 per scramble;32 scrambles for initial empirical curves;128 for uncertain slope/tail diagnostics only if the idea survives. Seeds derive from case/method/replicate labels, avoiding seed selection. Freeze the covariance eigenbasis, vectorization/batching, controls, conditioning direction and root-solve tolerance. Keep D1/D2 only as regression fixtures.

First classical menu: controlled RQMC-PCA; Brownian bridge; leading-factor preintegration with PCA and CAS; conditional integration and antithetics; optimized/vectorized CPU and available GPU. Include quadrature/sparse-grid/low-rank alternatives when effective dimension or empirical ranks warrant them. Plain MC is a correctness baseline. MLMC/MLRQMC becomes mandatory when a changed stochastic-volatility model introduces costly time refinement; there is no reason to manufacture that cost in exact GBM transitions.

Separate two output contracts. The diagnostic contract uses empirical intervals across independent scrambles and a reference from independent high-budget conditional RQMC plus a different construction, with reference uncertainty at most epsilon/10. The formal contract uses bounded integrands/residuals, a proved tail allowance, independent-scramble betting/empirical-Bernstein confidence and deterministic numerical bounds. Do not compare formal QAE confidence with an unlabeled empirical classical interval, nor force both methods into an unnecessarily weak formal procedure. Charge preintegration's root-solving cost and preprocessing.

Cheapest decisive step: measure classical time-to-tolerance and insert an optimistic *lower bound for the chosen quantum architecture*, not an arbitrary optimistic gate duration, into the latency inequality. Stop the candidate if even this cannot reach10-fold. Continue to a reversible Sobol/path/payoff prototype only if a meaningful margin remains. If a lower bound is unavailable, report maximum permissible tau_A and compile the oracle to resolve it; do not declare a win.

Confirmation: freeze implementation, parameter cases, tolerance allocation and hardware scenarios before opening held-out results. Require10-fold lower total latency on at least80% of held-out cases, success on every required accuracy/confidence contract, and no reliance on one favorable correlation/strike corner. Report all failures, memory/physical-qubit caps, full costs, uncertainty and sensitivity to10-fold changes in oracle duration. A narrower advantage region requires a new declared confirmation set, not retrospective relabeling.

Week1 deliverable: costed classical kill screen and permissible-oracle-latency surface, at most8 CPU-hours initially with batched memory under8GiB; stop before circuit engineering if classical results leave no feasible margin. If it passes, weeks2–3 compile/error-budget a coherent oracle; week4 compare all classical finalists and independently validate reference/tails; weeks5–6 freeze and execute held-out confirmation plus physical factory/cycle sensitivity. Dependencies are an actually viable first-stage margin and access to a defensible architecture model.

## Claim ledger and next action

Established locally: toy linear-family midpoint/antithetic exactness and full-shot arithmetic accounting. Established literature: coherent Sobol construction; classical conditional QMC techniques; bounded-replicate nonasymptotic confidence constructions. Conditional projections: permissible oracle times and finite-window crossover algebra. Hypothesis: an actual financial oracle can satisfy those times over a held-out region. Unsupported: QqMC practical advantage from the current toy plots; high nominal dimension as evidence of hardness; a pricing advantage from the Hermite transform alone; novelty from simply combining Sobol preparation, controls and QAE.

**The single next action for this route is to benchmark conditional RQMC/CAS at the intended price tolerance and compute the permitted full coherent-oracle latency.** It can reject a costly QqMC implementation effort before that effort begins.
