# Errata for the 23 September investigation and H1 falsifier

24 September 2026. Four independent critics reviewed the paper plan (see
`manuscript/advantage-frontier-2026-09-23/reviews/PLAN_REVIEW_R0.md`). They found the
errors below in documents committed on 23 September (commit `47706a21`). Each was checked
against the archived artifacts before being accepted. **None reverses the STOP decision
under its own stated rule** (continue only if some region with ε ≥ $0.001, t ≥ 100 ns,
k ≥ 3 fits). Several change how robustness and evidence strength were described. The
affected sentences have been corrected in place, and this file records what changed.

| ID | What was stated | What the artifacts show | Direction | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | "Extreme corner … still falls 1.7–12× short" (H1_FALSIFIER_RESULTS; WHY_NO_ADVANTAGE §4) | That covered only ε ∈ {$0.01, $0.001}, the two values `barrier_decision.py` prints. Across the 192-row grid, 19 rows have oracle/D_max ≤ 10, and one fits: 8×52, ε = $0.10, k = 1, 10 ns, free Gaussians, preintegrated σ (ratio 0.92). All 19 need a 10 ns T-layer and/or k = 1, outside the stop rule's admissible region | Overstated robustness | Reworded: robustness is stated at the decision point, and joint corners are listed explicitly |
| E2 | "A literature-best / Chakrabarti-class oracle (~10⁴) is still 13–65× too deep" | True only at the decision point ($0.001, 100 ns, k = 3). The 9.5×10³ figure is Chakrabarti et al.'s per-operator depth for a different, smaller payoff; it is not a floor | Overstated | Qualified to "at the decision point"; relabelled as a hypothetical oracle, not a floor |
| E3 | "Minimal compiled knock-out oracle", "certified reversible IR", "every choice minimized depth" | The oracle was built in the project IR and **scored** with a per-operation table (cheapest or median certified leaf of each type, any operand range, dependency-only, unlimited parallelism). The table undercounts non-power-of-two constant multiplies and cosine lookups (by roughly 1.4× overall). No optimality was tested | Mislabelled; the number favours quantum | Reworded as a depth-optimized, leaf-table-scored oracle with an optimistic score |
