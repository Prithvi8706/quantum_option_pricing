Resource/timing assumptions
Preparation is paid, even if scheduled offline. No overlap is assumed
without an explicit dependency/layout certificate. For each interface the
ledger records Clifford operations, T resources, readout, preparation,
feedback, routing and peak workspace separately. Known base Bell resource
qubits are 2 per T if all are prepared simultaneously; resource consumption
is distinct from a peak footprint. Additional selective-cell resources are
unknown. Clifford throughput, measurement acquisition, transport, decoder,
conditional dispatch, code-cycle duration and all physical errors are null.
Conventional latency includes Clifford/surgery, injection measurement,
decode/feedback and materialized correction. Candidate latency includes
prepared Clifford/Bell/selective resources plus all online basis decisions
and terminal frame interpretation. Their maxima can be lower bounds only;
no compatible executable time is claimed.
