F12 initial S4 coupling and boundary preflight

Frozen S4 has kappa2,theta=v0.04,xi.5,rho-.7, four assets, twelve dates.
Its Feller ratio2*kappa*theta/xi^2 is16/25=.64. The accessible zero boundary
does not imply its existing discrete variance scheme produces negative values.
The current update numerator equals
(sqrt(v)+xi*dw/2)^2+(kappa*theta-xi^2/4)h.
Here the last coefficient is7/400>0, and the denominator1+kappa*h>0.
Thus the current scheme is already positive for every v>=0 and real dw.

The one nominated alternative is Alfonsi2005 equation4, in the primary
author PDF https://cermics.enpc.fr/~alfonsi/SC_preprint.pdf, pp3-4.
For Y=sqrt(v), Ito's formula away from zero gives drift
(kappa*theta-xi^2/4)/(2Y)-kappa*Y/2 and noise xi*dW/2.
Its implicit drift step solves A*y^2-B*y-a*h=0, where
A=1+kappa*h/2>0, B=sqrt(v)+xi*dw/2 and a=7/800>0.
The roots have opposite signs and discriminant B^2+4*A*a*h>B^2; hence
there is exactly one positive root for every increment and every frozen h.
This algebra gives positivity. It neither proves that discrete transitions
are exact CIR transitions nor gives a strong or weak convergence rate.

The existing coarse path sums consecutive Brownian increments and the
antithetic path swaps them. A candidate must keep the original correlated
stock/variance Gaussian vectors, all twelve contractual fixings and this
fine/coarse coupling. Two independent h increments have variance2h and
stock/variance cross covariance2*rho*h. Coupling a marginal exact CIR draw
to an unrelated stock normal would lose the required joint law.

The primary paper's convergence results have additional hypotheses and
test-function domains. Proposition3.1 requires its (HS) scheme hypothesis
and gives logarithmic univariate variance-process bounds, not beta=2.
Proposition4.2 requires (HW), a C4 terminal test function and polynomial
fourth-derivative growth; it concerns a single CIR endpoint. A raw Asian
positive-part payoff has an exercise kink and also uses the coupled stock
and date average. No theorem for its conditional smoothed version has been
instantiated here. Its positivity result is not a beta=2 theorem for
this coupled multidimensional Heston Asian. At zero sqrt(v) lacks bounded
derivatives; its inverse-moment and full equity/payoff error obligations
remain open. The implicit Y drift is singular there. The existing rational
Milstein variance update also has conditional mean(v+kappa*theta*h)/(1+kappa*h),
while exact CIR has theta+(v-theta)*exp(-kappa*h); these differ off v=theta.

Levels1,2,3,4 are fixed for an eventual matched diagnostic. Current historical
S4 beta observations near1.24 remain descriptive fits with no confidence band.
No path acquisition is performed in this initial audit: full equity weak
bias, reference accuracy and matched timing are unresolved. Finite algebra
controls at those four h values validate interface signs/covariance only.
No 20-percent cost-times-variance result or complete F12 solution is claimed.
