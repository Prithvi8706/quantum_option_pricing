The one fixed case is existing A1: kappa2,theta=v0.09,xi.3,rho-.5,
S0=K100,r.03,T1, twelve dates. These are not the S4 parameters.

For h=1/12, q=exp(-kappa*h), c=xi^2(1-q)/(4*kappa),
nu=4*kappa*theta/xi^2=8 and lambda=q*v/c, the CIR endpoint has law
c*chi-square_noncentral(nu,lambda). Its mean is q*v+theta*(1-q),
and variance xi^2*v*q*(1-q)/kappa+theta*xi^2*(1-q)^2/(2*kappa).
Substitution into c(nu+lambda) and2*c^2(nu+2lambda) proves these identities.
For this integer nu only, an endpoint can use one shifted squared normal
plus seven independent squared normals. Eight Gaussian draws per interval
give96 at twelve dates. This counts real-distribution primitive draws,
not emitted loading circuits. q,lambda,square root, finite Gaussian loading,
quantization and cleanup are not free. General noninteger nu would require
a different central-chi-square/Gamma or Poisson-mixture implementation.

With I=integral_v over the interval and variance endpoints v,v', integration
of the CIR SDE gives J=integral_sqrt(v)dW=(v'-v-kappa*theta*h+kappa*I)/xi.
Conditional on these variance data, the independent stock driver gives
log(S'/S)=r*h-I/2+rho*J+sqrt((1-rho^2)*I)*Z.
This identity accommodates the original negative rho. Omitting rho*J changes
the law. A further independent equity Gaussian at each date is paid.

The remaining conditional I law requires the primary conditional transform,
with complex square-root branch gamma(a)=sqrt(kappa^2-2i*xi^2*a), modified
Bessel function order nu/2-1=3, Fourier quadrature and CDF inversion. Its
tail, quadrature, special-function and inverse errors must be enclosed for
the actual endpoint domain. An endpoint transition alone, or replacing I
with a trapezoidal mean of v and v', does not provide that certificate.

If a capped payoff is bounded by B and transition total-variation errors are
eta_j, a sequential coupling gives dollar error<=B sum eta_j. This requires
actual numerical eta_j bounds; they are not zero. The uncapped contract needs
its separately charged tail and path-loading/stock error bounds. No standalone
tail proof closes conditional I lower endpoints or all transition primitives.

The frozen run validates endpoint moment algebra and leverage identities,
with no random paths and no empirical conditional-moment/price interval.
The law inventory is complete at that scope. An end-to-end classical transition
pilot is stopped on missing conditional-I inverse accuracy, as the plan permits;
its price comparison and complete cost remain unresolved. All twelve dates,
special-function repetitions, preparation, inverses, arithmetic, readout and
precision are explicit obligations. No emitted quantum transition, cheaper
delivered source, A4 covariance extension or whole F14 completion is claimed.
