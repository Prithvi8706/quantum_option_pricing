# E25 adaptive amplitude coverage audit

Primary design inspected: [Ramoa–Santos2412.04394v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04394v5), entire locally extracted main text and AppendicesA–J; equations9,19,20,39 and Algorithms1,2,8 were checked. It proposes Bayesian posterior updates, greedy utility selection, particle approximations and noise learning. Reported scaling/error comparisons are numerical; this inspected design does not supply a uniform frequentist stopping-coverage certificate. Its finite particle/prior, model calibration and controller errors therefore remain obligations. Our bounded diagnostic specializes its likelihood/update/expected-variance operations to a rational amplitude grid and four allowed controls, rather than replaying its SMC implementation or benchmarks.

One own wrapper is sufficient to give mathematical coverage for adaptive clean amplitude experiments. Let F_t be the observed histories, m_t predictable from F_(t-1), and D_t conditionally Bernoulli q_(m_t)(a), where a in [0,1] is fixed. A clean shot of G^m A costs m+1 A forwards, m inverses and m each of zero/good reflections, plus one measurement/reset. Assume the conditional law holds for every history; fresh shots, no unmodelled noise or informative losses. For any proposed amplitude b let L_t(b)=product_(s<=t) q_(m_s)(b)^D_s[1-q_(m_s)(b)]^(1-D_s). Let pi be a fixed probability distribution, Z_t=sum_b pi_b L_t(b), and E_t(a)=Z_t/L_t(a) along histories possible under a, starting at1. The chosen control is identical in all likelihoods at the realized history; it is not reoptimized for each candidate.

For each b and a with true positive probabilities, conditional expectation of the likelihood-ratio increment is sum_(d:trueprob(d)>0) prob_b(d)<=1. At a zero-probability outcome the history has P_a mass0; define E there arbitrarily (the rejection rule below remains explicit). Thus each ratio, and their fixed nonnegative mixture, is a nonnegative supermartingale. If tau is its first crossing of1/alpha, the bounded-time stopped expectation is at most1, so P_a(tau<=n)<=alpha for every n. Monotone convergence of crossing events gives P_a(exists t:E_t(a)>=1/alpha)<=alpha. This proves the wrapper without prior coverage assumptions and without using simulations. [Ramdas et al., SAVI2023](https://glennshafer.com/assets/downloads/articles/article112_gametheoretic-sts894.pdf), Sections2.5/2.8 Eq8–12, supplies the corresponding primary Ville/inversion framework. This is an amplitude likelihood wrapper; it does not alter protected E12 residual confidence rules.

Consequently C_t={a in[0,1]:L_t(a)>alpha Z_t} has simultaneous coverage at least1-alpha, and the same holds for its intersection over past times or a conservative outer hull at any adapted stopping time. For our grid prior a=1/2 has positive weight and q_m(1/2)=1/2 for every allowed m, so Z_t>0 for all finite histories. If a likelihood is zero we reject it, but that history has zero probability under that a. The implementation uses the complete implicit continuous likelihood set and outward interval cells; it never calls a prior's finite support a confidence interval. Posterior credibility is a different object.

For m=0,1,2,3 the likelihoods are polynomials q_m(a)=a*P_m(a)^2 with P0=1, P1=3-4a, P2=5-20a+16a², P3=7-56a+112a²-64a³. They follow from the multiple-angle sine identity. Every fixed realized history therefore gives an exact polynomial L_t on[0,1]. Exact rational interval Horner bounds on each of256 closed dyadic cells enclose all its values. A cell is excluded only when its upper bound<=alpha Z_t. All others are retained; their hull encloses C_t, including all disconnected alias branches. Empty or wide hulls return UNCERTIFIED at the four-shot cap. An interval midpoint estimate is allowed only after a nonempty hull width<=2/64; then the confidence event ensures error<=1/64. Any later numerical readout must preserve the exact dyadic midpoint or charge rounding separately.

The bounded design uses a fixed uniform13-point amplitude prior, one unamplified warm-up, then at most three exact single-shot greedy controls minimizing posterior variance in a. Utility weights sum over both possible outcomes; zero predictive-probability contributions are zero and skipped without normalizing an impossible posterior. Ties choose the smaller m. This is a deterministic finite diagnostic specialization; no random particle resampling or continuous-posterior approximation is attributed to it. Eighteen fixed truths include small amplitudes, all endpoints, exact quarter alias boundaries and nearby off-prior values. Full binary-history enumeration computes exact all-look rejection probabilities and admitted-hull containment. These are law checks, not a universal coverage proof or device trials. The symbolic proof above supplies coverage for every a, including off-prior values and arbitrary predictable control selection under the model.

At m1, q1(0)=q1(3/4)=0 and q1(1/4)=q1(1)=1. A posterior single-mode choice can hide these branches; our likelihood set keeps them until data support exclusion. This diagnoses that literal reporting rule only, and does not claim a general Bayesian theorem failure.
