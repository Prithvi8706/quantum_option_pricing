Regret r=(C-K)+-d(C-K) is nonnegative. If the decision is wrong, K lies
between p and C, so r=|C-K|<=|C-p|; otherwise r=0.
For any certified L<=C<=U the pointwise envelope is (K-L)+ if d=1
and (U-K)+ if d=0. It vanishes when the interval and policy agree entirely
on one side of K. Exercise at equality may follow either convention without
nonzero regret. An interval merely crossing K gives no certified decision.
