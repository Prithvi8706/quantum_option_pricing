# Publication decision

Date: 2026-09-21. **Conditional go for drafting a narrow certified comparative
study; no go for a primitive/theory-breakthrough or quantum-advantage paper.**
This is an AI-assisted scientific judgment. It is not human novelty clearance,
journal endorsement, a submission decision or a prediction of acceptance.

## Strongest paper the evidence supports today

Working title: **Classical controls change logical-resource comparisons in
quantum Asian-basket pricing: a certified case study.**

Central question: when two quantum payoff constructions solve the same
financial problem, how do control matching, deterministic error, discrete AE
schedules and workspace affect their resource ordering?

The main contribution is a reproducible comparison and its error/resource
explanation. A concrete shared-reflection signal and certified reversible
arithmetic/control implementation make that comparison possible. The D2
reversal corrects a weaker comparator and demonstrates a material consequence;
it is not discovery of the residual-estimation principle.

**Proposed central claim:**

> For two specified development Asian-basket contracts, we implement and
> certify reflection/QSP and reversible-arithmetic pricing constructions
> under a common ideal-logical dollar-error and confidence contract. Giving
> arithmetic a comparable degree-four classical control changes the lowest
> declared logical-CX route in D2, while reflection remains lowest in D1.
> The D2 reflection/residual ratio is 2.9346 with control cancellation and
> 3.2892 in the conservative ledger, at 103 versus 5,162 allocated qubits.
> Complete menus, source-bound evidence and independent replay expose the
> normalization, query and workspace tradeoffs behind this local finding.

The manuscript must attach the exact compiler/decomposition model, selected
plans and finite-menu qualification to this claim. It must specify whether
it uses the frozen exact-label convention or the new certified classical
label adapter. It must never convert projected ratios into runtime speedups.

## Claims available now

- A correct application-specific construction and carefully bounded numerical
  composition, with the mathematical restrictions in the audit.
- Reproduced D1/D2 ordering within the frozen menu and two logical ledgers.
- A same-parent explanation: added control can increase per-A cost while
  reducing normalization and total AE demand enough to win.
- Large allocated-width cost, discrete schedule sensitivity, retained failures
  and duplicate nominal-degree limitations.
- Recoverable source/evidence and a repaired classical decoding interface.

These are computational findings, not fresh statistical confirmation on new
contracts. Existing development/evaluation classifications remain unchanged.

## One bounded follow-up before a stronger manuscript claim

Perform the [common-basis selected-oracle study](BOUNDED_FOLLOWUP_PLAN.md).
Fix one compilation/optimization policy and compare the already selected D1/D2
reflection, raw-parent and residual oracles, including controls/inverses and
all allocated ancillas. Recompute schedules only from the same certified
target contract. Report whether the ordering and width frontier survive.
This resolves the most consequential actionable fairness uncertainty without
changing the application or launching an advantage search.

Drafting the present model-specific case study can begin now. A claim about
the ranking under a shared compilation policy must wait for that follow-up;
even success would not prove compiler-independent or globally optimal costs.
A qualified human should then assess whether the resulting
consequence is sufficiently original and useful. The self-contained packet is
prepared; nobody has been contacted or credited with agreement.

## What requires substantial new research

End-to-end quantum-over-classical runtime advantage requires a physical
execution/synthesis/error-correction model, complete classical preprocessing
costs, and a genuinely matched strong classical target/coverage comparison.
Generality beyond D1/D2 requires prospectively chosen cases and a justified
scaling argument. Globally optimal arithmetic, distribution-aware QSP,
improved loaders or a new asymptotic theorem require additional algorithms
and proofs. These are not small remaining checklist items and are not
prerequisites for honestly documenting the current local comparison.

## Claims to abandon or explicitly exclude

New reflection/LCU/QSP primitive; first signed-payoff encoder; new
classical-polynomial-plus-quantum-residual principle; globally optimal
normalization for actual correlated support; universal best encoding;
universal crossover; optimized/hardware runtime ratios; measured quantum
advantage; physical $1 delivery; fresh confirmation; human expert approval;
publication/submission readiness. The finite-menu minimum is not a new
optimization theorem, and 166 rows are not 166 independent circuits.

The research direction should stay with the existing financial target.
The least unnecessary change is to the **paper's claim**, from broad quantum
method novelty to a certified comparator-sensitivity result. If the common
comparison or human significance review is unfavorable, use the reproducible
technical-report fallback rather than repeatedly tuning until a favorable
headline appears.
