## Agreement, disagreement and unresolved scope

All three reviewers recommend the narrow comparative/reproducibility framing
and reject new-primitive or advantage claims. This agreement is recorded, not
manufactured human consensus. They emphasize different threats: prior-art
overlap, numerical delivery interface, and common compilation/width fairness.
The initial v1 minimality phrasing was a concrete disagreement resolved by a
stronger bound. The initial concern that the label gap might invalidate the
headline became a scoped distinction: frozen ideal-label projections remain;
binary64 delivery must opt into the new adapter and allowance.

Unresolved: publication significance/priority after human scrutiny; strongest
external arithmetic; common-policy compilation; physical errors/runtime;
rigorous matched classical coverage; generality outside the development menu.
No review is an all-code or all-literature audit. Existing tests/replays were
not rerun wholesale; historical receipts and fresh checks are distinguished.
The reviewers' reports retain their own materials/limits rather than replacing
them with a blanket “approved by experts” statement.
