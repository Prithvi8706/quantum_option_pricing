# Search process and limits

Actual search date: **2026-09-21**. Searches used primary arXiv records/full text,
publisher pages, author-uploaded manuscripts, author institutional records,
Crossref publisher deposits and official IEEE QCE accepted-paper lists.
Search snippets were discovery aids; mathematical conclusions use inspected
methods, assumptions or theorem passages. No researchers were contacted,
services purchased or external implementations/hardware run.

- Manzano et al.'s preprint now has a published 2025 version under a changed
  title. Fedoriaka et al.'s arXiv record now supplies QCE2025 publication and DOI.
- The four recent arXiv identifiers in the historical W2 note all exist.
  N9/N10 had relevant methods inspected. **2605.26610v1** (Guseynov et al.,
  26 May 2026) and **2606.00458v1** (Greenwell et al., submitted 30 May 2026)
  were screened from primary metadata/abstracts only. Neither is a reproduced
  or formula-audited competitor in this assessment.
- N9's plotted effective query budget omits repeated shots; the full count
  is separately specified by its authors. N10's complexity theorem assumes
  loading/rotation oracles. These limits prevent importing headline advantages.
- The root checked K1's actual author-uploaded methods rather than relying on
  the old repository citation. Its publication priority remains preprint-level.

## Access limits and exclusions

IEEE DOI retrieval failed for the Cibrario papers. Their official acceptance
and publisher/institutional metadata were available, and full author preprints
were read. Page-identical final proceedings text was not verified. SIAM
metadata was accessible; the detailed formulas were inspected in author
preprints, not asserted identical to the final publisher PDFs. The existence
of associated code is recorded separately from execution; no external code
comparison was performed during this task.

Tutorials, marketing pages, social posts, aggregators, patents discovered by
search, and AI-generated summaries were excluded as algorithmic evidence.
Physics quantum-Monte-Carlo sign-problem control variates concern a different
computational setting and were not treated as direct gate-QAE pricing prior art.
Jiaxin Yu/Xiaoqun Wang's [2603.01763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01763) was
abstract-screened as a future classical-comparator lead only.

Unresolved coverage: unpublished industrial implementations, inaccessible
revisions, non-English work and papers missed by targeted queries. No absence
of an identical title, theorem label or application example establishes
novelty. Qualified human review remains desirable for significance and any
priority claim. Venue selection was deliberately deferred; this report makes
no current fee, quartile, acceptance or submission-policy assertion.
