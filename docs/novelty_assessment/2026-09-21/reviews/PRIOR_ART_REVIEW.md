## Most consequential findings

1. **The polynomial offset/residual idea has a direct, old quantum precedent.** Novak's *Quantum Complexity of Integration*, §3, equation (25), uses a classically integrated approximation plus quantum integration of its residual. The project may contribute an explicit finance-specific control oracle and its certificates, but not this decomposition. The source's smoothness-class complexity theorem does not automatically apply to our kinked payoff or weighted finite grid. [Novak, 2001](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0008124v3).

2. **The signed shift and comparator are established.** Stamatopoulos–Zeng §4, equations (20)–(27), supplies uniform-register comparison, normalization and a shift for negative inputs. Mapping its shifted input to our `residual_int + H` makes the primitive overlap explicit. Our overflow proof and exact-integer threshold implementation are useful specialization work; adding an affine decoder is not a new encoding. [Stamatopoulos–Zeng, 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.14310v2#S4).

3. **QSP replacing payoff arithmetic is already a published contribution category.** The project's separable-basket signal differs from transforming a digitally represented scalar log return. That can make the implementation useful; it does not establish priority for QSP finance, arithmetic avoidance, or applying amplitude estimation to a payoff. The shared-reflection identity and LCU normalization in the project's Proposition 1 remain immediate applications of established unitary/LCU facts, as the proposition already acknowledges.

4. **Generic normalization-aware resource choice is also established.** Cibrario et al. explicitly relate probability precision to payoff normalization and show that poor rescaling can erase a shallower amplitude-loader benefit. Thus a paper cannot present “minimize query count times oracle cost, accounting for normalization” as the new general idea. [Cibrario et al., QCE 2025, §§III–IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.19039v1#S3).

5. **Asian pricing without nested AE is not a valid novelty distinction.** Prakash et al. include a coherent running-average alternative followed by final AE in §5, equations (70)–(73), in addition to the nested construction. Different precision and distribution assumptions preclude importing its asymptotic resource bounds into D1/D2. [Prakash et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10132v1#S5).

6. **Complete error accounting itself is not an unoccupied field.** Herman et al.'s February 2026 preprint gives an explicit pricing-error framework and improved GBM quadrature analysis. The distinguishing value here must be executable directed certificates and matched artifacts for this particular construction, not the first framework coupling truncation, discretization, arithmetic, loading and AE error. [Herman et al., §§4.2–4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03725v1#S4.SS2).

## Interpretation of the reversal

The reversal is scientifically informative as a caution about comparator design. It is largely explained by giving arithmetic a control that the reflection route already used: the residual's smaller normalization permits fewer AE calls, at the cost of a larger oracle and far greater allocated width. The historical raw-arithmetic comparison is not fraudulent; its original comparison set was weaker and its preferred-route interpretation has been superseded.

The selected raw and residual rows also change precision, so their headline ratio is not a causal estimate for centering alone. The same-precision parent comparison requested during this review can be reconstructed from existing D1_28/D2_28 archives; the resource reviewer is handling it. Agreement between two CX ledgers strengthens the narrow result but does not convert projections into actual runtime bounds. A roughly 50-fold D2 width increase makes a hardware recommendation premature.

“Case-dependent winner” by itself is unsurprising. The added value would be explaining, certifying and exposing the crossover mechanism so that another researcher can reproduce or challenge it. Two development cases establish examples, not a general crossover law, an out-of-sample success rate or fresh confirmation.

## Publication disposition

**Conditional go for a carefully scoped comparative methods/artifact paper; no-go for a new-primitive, general speedup or quantum-advantage paper on present evidence.** A technical report containing the exact current empirical claim can be prepared now. A stronger publication claim needs one credible external comparator or an equally consequential independently checked demonstration that the result is not an artifact of this arithmetic implementation. Qualified human assessment remains pending.

Recommended central claim:

> We provide reproducible, error-budgeted logical-resource comparisons of reflection/QSP and reversible arithmetic for specified arithmetic Asian-basket contracts. Matching a classical polynomial control changes the preferred projected-CX route in one of two development cases and exposes a substantial allocation-versus-gate-count tradeoff.

Use “error-budgeted” subject to the final disposition of the separate mathematical audit; this review is not certification of the executable decoder. Do not claim the first use of residual/control QAE, first signed-payoff encoding, first QSP option pricing, universal optimal normalization for correlated baskets, a globally cheapest encoding, a hardware speedup, or established publication-level novelty.

Minimum consequential work: complete the numerical interface repair identified by the mathematical review; retain the same-precision parent ablation; specify one external arithmetic implementation and compile it under the same contract/precision/error/gate conventions; document the width tradeoff and a small predetermined sensitivity menu if making a broader crossover claim. Do not expand into Heston, PDE pricing or quantum QMC merely to acquire a novelty label. No unbounded advantage search is warranted.
