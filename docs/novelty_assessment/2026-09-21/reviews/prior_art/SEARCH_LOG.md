## Access and coverage limits

- ArXiv HTML/PDF and the EPJ publisher full text were accessible. When browser extraction truncated long passages, direct primary-source downloads were read locally. No inaccessible article was inferred from a snippet.
- IEEE DOI navigation for the QCE25 paper failed in the browser tool. Its arXiv full text was read; official conference acceptance and publisher-deposited Crossref metadata were verified. Exact identity of preprint and proceedings text remains unchecked.
- The project's historical July 2026 Fourier-arithmetic ResearchGate upload was not newly formula-reviewed in this scope. Its historical report is not promoted to independent verification. The resource review addresses external arithmetic alternatives separately.
- Query coverage is deliberately bounded, not systematic-review completeness or novelty clearance. No proprietary implementation, unpublished manuscript, complete patent search or exhaustive citation graph was examined.
- “No journal version established” records search outcome, not proof of non-publication. Four recent peripheral preprints do not substitute for reading the closest older work.
- No source author was contacted. No service was purchased, hardware job run or external repository code executed.
