# Referee report

Date 2026-09-21. AI-assisted assessment of the repository at the verified
takeover source plus the separately versioned assessment overlay. This is not
an actual journal report or human expert endorsement.

**Recommendation: major revision of the scientific framing; conditional go
for a narrow computational-methods case study.** The code/evidence package is
substantial. The broad novelty case is weak. The central publishable question
is whether the fully explained comparator correction teaches a reproducible
and useful lesson beyond standard residual estimation.

## Major issues

1. **The apparent new ingredients have direct precedents.** Classical
   approximation plus quantum residual has an explicit predecessor in Novak;
   shifted comparator encoding appears in Stamatopoulos–Zeng. QSP pricing,
   reversible Horner, clean-workspace tradeoffs and resource-driven selection
   also predate the project. The manuscript must not create novelty by
   renaming them. The claim matrix supplies exact mappings and restrictions.
2. **D2's reversal chiefly repairs comparator fairness.** Giving both routes
   comparable controls is scientifically necessary. The useful new evidence
   is the magnitude/mechanism and width cost of the resulting local ranking,
   not the fact that a control can reduce query demand. The exact-parent
   ablation substantially improves the explanation; retain it prominently.
3. **The compilation model limits numerical interpretation.** Both ledgers
   agree, but they share assumptions and are not independent hardware models.
   Component decomposition, optimized reflection circuits and streamed
   arithmetic do not prove a common optimized implementation ratio. A single
   bounded common-policy comparison is the most valuable next experiment.
4. **Practical significance is unestablished.** D2's 375 billion projected CX
   and 5,162 wires are large; reflection uses 103 wires. Strong classical
   conditional RQMC already works well on the chosen low-dimensional targets.
   Classical approximate intervals and ideal quantum confidence cannot be
   described as a rigorous matched race. Remove any advantage rhetoric.
5. **Generality is limited.** Two development cases and a fixed menu do not
   establish a generally preferred encoding. Nominal degree 12/16 entries
   frequently implement the same quantized polynomial. A minimum over the
   table is not a new decision theorem. The larger menus and older failed
   cases remain context, not independent confirmation of this new comparison.
6. **One executable numerical interface was incomplete.** The frozen
   residual decoder assumes a binary64 amplitude; exact AE-label conversion
   needed its own bound. The new opt-in adapter and independent check resolve
   this bounded issue without changing schedules. The manuscript must cite
   the new condition and must not claim the old producer included it.
7. **The theorem contribution should be modestly presented.** Independent-cube
   sharpness does not imply correlated-instance sharpness; proxy optimization
   is elementary; upper-projection ratios do not bound true cost ratios.
   These results should establish correctness and interpretation, not headline
   a fundamental advance.

## Strengths that survive those objections

The matched financial target is explicit. Signed rounding, overflow,
normalization versus bias, offset restoration and per-basket units are treated
carefully. Full menus and failures survive; evidence is source-bound, replay
coverage is stated accurately and unfavorable results are not suppressed.
Independent checks reproduce the central resource ledgers and residual bounds.
The comparison identifies query normalization as the driver even when the
controlled oracle becomes more expensive. That is a concrete, teachable result.

## Minor presentation and reproducibility requirements

Always label the gate basis, ledger and ratio direction. Place allocated width
next to gate counts. Separate primary dependency depth from residual serial
depth bounds. Describe the 1,645/315 test receipts as overlapping historical
runs and the new checks separately. Preserve coefficient-equivalent rows but
report unique map counts. Do not call source-producer replay an independent
proof. Distinguish a discretely monitored contract with continuous Gaussian
inputs from a continuously monitored option. Keep the old Paper A draft and
its stale publication claims clearly historical.

## Disposition

No fatal contradiction was found in the narrowed ideal-logical comparison
within the reviewers' stated scopes. Direct prior art is fatal to several
broad novelty claims, and missing physical/classical comparison is fatal to
an advantage paper today. The narrow comparison may support a research article
after the bounded fairness check and a clear manuscript, but its significance
still deserves qualified human scrutiny. A reproducible technical report is
an honest fallback if that judgment is unfavorable. Further random tuning
would not resolve the main objections.
