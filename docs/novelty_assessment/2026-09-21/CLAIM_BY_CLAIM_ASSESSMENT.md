# Claim-by-claim assessment

Date 2026-09-21. Confidence here describes the strength of this AI assessment,
not a statistical probability of originality or acceptance. Source IDs refer
to the [bibliography](PRIMARY_SOURCE_BIBLIOGRAPHY.md); see the
[nearest-method matrix](NEAREST_PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.md) for formula locations.

## A. New quantum primitive — unsupported

**Candidate claim:** shared reflection, a signed selector, or its decoder is a
new primitive. **Known:** PREP/SELECT, signed LCU and projected reflections
are established in F5/F6; N2 explicitly supplies shifted comparator encoding.
**Addition:** a concrete multiplexer arrangement for this separable basket,
with a certificate and implementation. **Assumptions:** positive exponential
row weights, bounded cube, correct reflection/involution and cleanup.
**Evidence:** W1 derivation, emitted circuits, small coherent checks.
**Falsifier of priority:** an earlier explicit identity already suffices;
such identities were found. **Significance:** useful application engineering.
**Paper role:** supporting construction only; a primitive paper is no-go.
Confidence in this exclusion is high.

## B. New algorithmic construction — narrow implementation distinction only

**Candidate claim:** classical polynomial offset plus quantum residual pricing,
or QSP pricing without inner AE, is new. **Known:** N1's §3 eq.(25) explicitly
integrates an approximation classically and its residual quantumly; N2 gives
QSP pricing; N4 §5 has a nonnested running-average alternative.
**Addition:** the separable Asian-basket realization and a matched signed-floor
control oracle. **Assumptions:** moments through degree four are tractable,
the control is paid for, its residual is universally bounded, and all target
bridges hold. **Evidence:** emitted control compute/uncompute and complete
archives. **Potential invalidation:** an earlier identical application
construction, or a materially cheaper conventional implementation.
**Significance:** application-specific, not a new general algorithm.
**Paper role:** potentially part of a comparative paper; insufficient alone.
Priority confidence remains low despite high confidence in the stated algebra.

## C. New theorem or meaningful extension — correctness results, limited novelty

**Candidate claim:** sharp normalization, optimized degree, or finite-menu
selection is a substantial new theorem. **Known:** contraction plus LCU gives
the independent-cube bound; elementary optimization gives the cost proxy;
an exhaustive argmin minimizes its supplied objective. **Addition:** explicit
domain restrictions and their connection to implemented error/cost ledgers.
**Assumptions:** independent cube (not actual correlated support), positive
weights, fixed constants for the continuous proxy, finite certified menu.
**Evidence:** [mathematical audit](MATHEMATICAL_AUDIT.md), exact counterexamples
to stronger readings, rational reconstructions. **Invalidation:** applying
the statements outside their hypotheses or finding a certificate defect.
**Significance:** correctness/methodology. **Paper role:** necessary support;
not enough for a theory-led paper. High confidence in this boundary.

## D. Useful application-specific construction — defensible, modest originality

**Claim:** an explicit shared-reflection separable lognormal-basket signal and
certified reversible residual oracle implement the stated finite target and
connect it to a dollar budget. **Known:** N2/N4/N7/N8 and R1/R2 implement related
pricing/arithmetic techniques. **Addition:** this exact signal, universal
signed/rounding/offset interfaces and reproducible composition.
**Assumptions:** frozen GBM representation; ideal logical execution; declared
precision/menu and source versions. **Evidence:** W1/W2, stronger-arithmetic
archives, all-row certificate checks and selected gate reconstructions.
**Invalidation:** omitted operations, an incorrect model bridge, or an earlier
equivalent full construction could reduce significance. **Significance:**
methodological and engineering. **Paper role:** viable supporting contribution;
moderate confidence in usefulness, no certified first-in-field claim.

## E. Rigorous decision rule or crossover criterion — useful screen, standard idea

**Claim:** choose the lowest declared logical CX among implementations that
meet the explicit deterministic/statistical budget. **Known:** R6 establishes
resource-driven design selection; N3 already explains normalization versus
query demand; the simplified calculus is elementary. **Addition:** explicit
refusal, discrete AE jumps, ties, open physical gates and reproducible choices.
**Assumptions:** sound input bounds, common units/target, fixed candidate menu.
**Evidence:** complete decisions and exact schedule/cost rechecks.
**Invalidation:** an uncharged cost, a sharper feasible alternative, or a
different hardware objective can change the choice. **Significance:**
methodological; the calibrated k=0 planner is separately useful but not AE.
**Paper role:** supporting decision framework, not standalone novelty.
No universal crossover or globally optimal encoding theorem follows.

## F. Substantive comparative finding — strongest candidate contribution

**Claim:** with a comparable classical control and complete stated ledgers,
D1 retains reflection as the lowest projected-CX route while D2 favors
signed-residual arithmetic, at much greater width. **Known:** oracle/query
tradeoffs and comparator sensitivity are expected in general. **Addition:**
a certified, replayable concrete counterexample to the project's earlier
reflection-over-raw-arithmetic interpretation, with exact matched-parent
decomposition and complete adverse/duplicate rows retained.
**Assumptions:** same two development contracts, fixed menu, ideal execution,
specified compiler/decomposition and both control conventions.
**Evidence:** all 166 rows; selected D2 ratios 2.9346/3.2892; same-parent
control ablation; 5,162 versus 103 qubits. **Invalidation:** a common compiler
or stronger arithmetic/reflective implementation can change the ranking; an
earlier equivalent comparison would narrow priority. **Significance:**
empirical/computational and methodological. **Paper role:** plausible central
result of a narrow study, conditional on clear scope and comparison checks.
High confidence in archived calculations; moderate in significance, low in
generalization. “We found a reversal” alone is insufficient novelty.

## G. Engineering and reproducibility — strong support, not novelty clearance

**Claim:** source-bound protocols, complete evidence, directed certificates,
recoverable failures, separate replays and test receipts make the finding
auditable. **Known:** these are established scientific-computing practices.
**Addition:** an unusually detailed executable package for this exact pricing
comparison, including a new certified classical AE-label adapter.
**Assumptions:** documented environment/source pins and respected replay scope.
**Evidence:** hashes/commit binding, independent resource and rational checks,
preservation inventory and versioned fix. **Invalidation:** irreproducible
checkout, missing dependencies/rights or mismatched hashes would weaken it.
**Significance:** engineering/methodological. **Paper role:** essential support;
a software/artifact or technical-report fallback is more credible than a
claim of foundational novelty. Tests and AI review do not prove publication
priority or supply human expert endorsement.

## H. Negative result or demonstrated limitation — defensible when local

**Claim:** component savings and raw-comparator wins do not determine the best
complete logical plan; width, control matching and error margins matter.
**Known:** full-cost qualifications predate us (R1, N3, F3/F4); two upper bounds
cannot order unknown optimized runtimes. **Addition:** concrete failures of
stronger interpretations, including an unfavorable tiny reflection circuit,
E2 menu refusal, D2 control correction and duplicate nominal degrees.
**Assumptions:** each observation retains its historical target/model.
**Evidence:** adverse rows and exact mathematical counterexamples.
**Invalidation:** broadening a local failure into a no-go theorem is itself
invalid; improved methods may remove it. **Significance:** methodological
negative evidence. **Paper role:** can strengthen the central comparative
story; no general impossibility result or refutation of quantum pricing.

## What can be said now

The strongest defensible contribution is **a reproducible certified case study
of control matching and encoding selection for quantum Asian-basket pricing**.
Its originality, if sufficient for publication, lies in the concrete evidence
and complete implementation comparison. The underlying primitives, residual
principle, conditional classical integration and generic selection logic are
useful but already known. The [publication decision](PUBLICATION_DECISION.md)
sets the exact proposed claim and minimum next check.
