## Decision

Position the candidate as an auditable reliability and cost study with explicit
refusal, not a new amplitude estimator or a successful adaptive controller.
The potentially distinctive combination is finite calibration, stipulated
transfer allowances, contract-dependent representation bounds and dollar-level
delivery accounting. Combining established ingredients is not, by itself,
evidence of journal-level novelty. The basket-paper methods comparison and a
matched native/classical evaluation remain substantive gaps, not tasks this
refresh can declare solved. Journal fees and policies were not rechecked here.
