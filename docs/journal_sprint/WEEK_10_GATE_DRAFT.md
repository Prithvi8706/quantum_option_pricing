# Proposed week 10 evidence gate

Historical proposal. Execution and disposition are now recorded in
[the week-10 closeout](WEEK_10_CLOSEOUT.md). This proposal itself was not a
frozen confirmation protocol.

1. Review the new manuscript against the newly screened basket-pricing paper's
   full methods, documenting exact overlap and genuinely unmatched requirements.
2. Predeclare a numerical stress matrix containing disconnected final intersections,
   near-critical branches, extreme probabilities and realistic large shot counts.
   Choose a validated high-precision strategy before running expensive beta tails;
   retain failures. Finite tests must still not be called formal certification.
3. Define one feasible matched native/classical comparison with consistent target,
   tolerance, failure allowance and complete cost ledger. Separate Bayesian
   posterior widths from frequentist guarantees. Do not launch an incomparable
   benchmark merely to fill a table.
4. Decide whether the evidence supports a reliability-methods paper without a
   successful controller, or requires a substantive methodological advance.
5. Review and log the decision before a frozen confirmation campaign. Keep
   submission, authorship verification, journal fee checks and Macroscope/PR as
   explicit later gates.

This is an evidence-driven next-stage proposal, not a promise of journal acceptance
or authorization to add contributors without genuine attributable contributions.
