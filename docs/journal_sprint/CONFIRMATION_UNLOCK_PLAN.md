## Next implementation: certified reversible arithmetic

Keep the product-normal registers and PCA log map. Start with a small test kernel
whose table can still be checked independently; the production construction must
not embed that table. Use signed fixed-point registers for Bz+mhat, approximate
exp on an explicitly bounded domain, add spots/average, compare to strike and
compute the nonnegative raw or geometric-control residual. Normalize using the
safe cube scale and encode the flag, then uncompute every temporary register.

Required artifacts before promotion:

1. Register layout, overflow/sign rules, exact coefficient encoding and
   coefficient/log-domain error propagation (including domain enlargement).
2. Polynomial/function approximation remainder plus fixed-point rounding bounds;
   a chosen32bit width is not evidence of accuracy.
3. Loading trace-distance allowance; arithmetic payoff error; normalized flag
   error; synthesis/operator error. Convert each to dollars with the actual safe
   scale and include inverse/uncompute errors. Do not treat small simulator
   discrepancy or a favorable observed sample as an upper-bound proof.
4. Small-circuit exhaustive truth/ancilla-return tests; nonzero Grover response
   tests; larger compile-only scaling with setup, A, A-inverse, reflections,
   gates/depth/qubits and fit/calibration costs in separate units.
5. A complete PriceContract with no unknown component and a checked total bias
   strictly below the chosen total price tolerance. This is representation
   admission only; statistical precision still must be delivered.

Explore QSP/function-loading only through an explicit circuit/error/cost
comparison with this kernel, not as an unimplemented promise. If safe scales and
precision costs remove any useful tradeoff, record that and visibly replan;
do not promote an arbitrarily chosen easier contract as the original problem.

## Then: like-for-like study and contribution test

Use exactly the same continuous contract, discretization meaning, control offset,
tail budget and paid setup for the classical and quantum comparisons. Report
finite-sum baselines when a table is used. Strong randomized-QMC uncertainty
comes from independent scrambles, not independent-point binomial assumptions.
Noise parameters must either be paid calibrated nuisance bounds in the method,
or an explicit ideal-model scope restriction. Known-noise synthetic experiments
cannot be renamed paid-noise-learning results.

Predeclare which method is primary only after development, before new holdouts.
If native IQAE is included without a verified stopping theorem for the exact
adapter, qualify its empirical intervals; do not use the fixed CP proof to
certify that different algorithm. Keep failed/capped/empty-output attempts in
their correct denominators and report unconditional and declaration-conditional
errors separately. All selection/pilot data and costs must be disclosed.

Novelty assessment should compare the complete error/cost-aware decision method
against known controls, known interval techniques and prior quantum derivative
resource studies. The new derivation assembles standard tools; it is not itself
evidence of a new quantum algorithm. The desired contribution must survive
strong baselines and independent review rather than be asserted from absence
of search matches or the word quantum in an implementation.

## A quantitative confirmation-design checkpoint

No confirmation observations were generated. For illustration, suppose10fixed
cells each have4one-sided reliability claims and familywise error allowance.05.
Bonferroni allocates alpha_local=.05/40=.00125; independence between claims is
not required. For zero Bernoulli events in n independent complete trials, the
one-sided exact binomial upper endpoint is1-alpha_local^(1/n).

The directed calculation in confirmation_zero_event_design_v1.json yields:

| Desired upper error rate | Minimum zero-event denominator | Enclosed upper endpoint |
|---|---:|---:|
| .05 | 131 | .049747529 |
| .01 | 666 | .009986753 |

At130and665the corresponding upper endpoints still exceed the targets. These
are **not** recommended fixed total sample sizes or a power calculation. Any
observed errors require a different binomial quantile calculation. A conditional
error claim uses the number of declarations, not all attempts; zero declarations
cannot establish that claim. Fixed total trial count, independent full-trial
calibrations where needed, anticipated delivery rate, uncertainty and joint
power requirements must be specified before choosing repetitions. No optional
early stopping is licensed by this zero-event formula.

The count of cells/claims, .05/.01targets, primary effect size and error budget
partition here are illustrative design inputs, not signed scientific decisions.
The16development repetitions cannot simply be relabeled adequate confirmation.

## Human review packet and final gate

Ask Aasa to review the quantum construction/resource assumptions and identify a
defensible distinction from prior work; give the statistical reviewer the full
error/stopping/selection argument and proposed outcome/multiplicity plan. Record
actual contributions, objections, resolutions, names and dates. Subagent review
is not their signature and cannot manufacture an authorship contribution.

Only after the numerical, oracle, fairness, contribution and review criteria
above are genuinely met should a versioned confirmation protocol be signed,
new seeds/held-out regimes unlocked, and the campaign run. Until then, report
development progress and the unresolved gates explicitly.
