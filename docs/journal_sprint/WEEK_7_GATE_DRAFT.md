The original weeks 7–10 main-experiment calendar is readiness-gated. Do not
spend the planned main matrix while the present validity/utility gates fail.

## Next scientific question

Which limitations respond to more pricing shots, which respond to calibration
size, and which remain under a stipulated transfer allowance? Week 6 only
varied the second while holding the allowance and pricing budget fixed.
Its rare E014/E025 declarations preclude a categorical impossibility claim.

Before any further observations, declare a bounded factorial ablation using
all C6, with separate axes for pricing budget, calibration size and *assumed*
guard width. A smaller guard is only valid in synthetic conditions whose actual
transfer it covers; it cannot be fitted to make saved results look better.
Retain center and boundary conditions, not only the favorable E001 corner.

An exact-known-readout diagnostic may help separate uncertainty sources, but
must be labeled an oracle diagnostic, never an implementable competitor or
input to a deployed controller. It requires its own prospective declaration.
No such ablation has been executed in week 6.

## Required design work

1. Choose a small explicit matrix, runtime/storage limits, confidence spending,
   and marginal-cost reporting before new draws. Preserve all refusals and
   separate interval noncontainment from erroneous declarations.
2. Verify matched-target interval/stopping semantics for modern comparator
   adapters. BAE/BIQAE source smokes alone remain inadequate head-to-head evidence.
   If native methods cannot support this noise/calibration claim, narrow the
   claim and comparison scope rather than presenting mismatched comparisons.
3. Specify useful-delivery and cost criteria before a new pilot/validation
   selector. Do not select representations, allowances or schedules from exact
   answers or recycle pilot counts as fresh validation observations.
4. Revisit the main-matrix go/no-go with explicit evidence. A narrower negative
   reliability study is possible, not automatically a novel journal contribution.

Human contributor approvals, numerical certification, realistic transfer-bound
justification and external review remain separate. Nothing here establishes
submission readiness. Macroscope/PR gate remains held until actually available.
