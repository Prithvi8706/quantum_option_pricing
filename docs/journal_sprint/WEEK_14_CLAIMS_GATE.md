## Week14 gate decision: NO-GO for week15 confirmation

The permitted finite-target development is complete, not the original conditional
end-to-end continuous-pricing milestone. Do not turn the absence of an admitted
encoding into invented price bounds. Remaining gates:

1. Componentwise valid truncation, discretization, payoff arithmetic and synthesis
   enclosures, with a non-enumerative coherent oracle or an explicitly defensible
   finite-target application. The current raw/residual contracts are unknown_bias.
2. A useful, defensible prior-work distinction: a theorem, scalable method, or
   substantive limitation study beyond this tiny known-control diagnostic.
3. Faithful same-target strong classical comparisons and paid setup/noise
   learning whenever those costs are claimed. Knowneta is not learnedeta.
4. Implementation-specific native coverage justification or narrower wording;
   fixed-model CP inference must not be substituted for native comparator claims.
5. Bounded human collaborator review and a genuinely prespecified confirmation
   protocol with primary outcomes, held-out regimes, new seeds, caps, precision/
   power and multiplicity plan. No human sign-off has been received or invented.

Variance-aware AE and coherent QMC remain research directions, not implemented
solutions to the measured table/bias bottlenecks. BAE/BIQAE Asian integration is
not claimed; their model/implementation applicability is in the comparator audit.
No fresh confirmation campaign or manuscript superiority claim is authorized
by this development closeout. Any route replacement needs a visible replan.
